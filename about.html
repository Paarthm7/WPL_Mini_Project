ABOUT URBAN GREEN

Building Smarter, Greener Cities

Urban Green is a smart urban infrastructure management platform designed to provide a centralized view of infrastructure projects and reported civic issues. The platform helps monitor development, track progress and identify infrastructure problems in urban areas.

Our Objective

The objective of Urban Green is to create a digital platform that makes urban infrastructure information easier to monitor and manage. It brings projects, infrastructure issues and progress information together in one place.

Why Urban Green?

Rapid urban development creates the need for better infrastructure monitoring. Urban Green provides a structured interface where infrastructure projects can be tracked while reported issues can be monitored based on their current status.
